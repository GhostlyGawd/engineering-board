The finishes are visibly differentiated and replace the original amber/cream

- **A1 Porcelain:** full serif hero, white/cool surfaces, black primary action,
  restrained blue source link. This is the strongest typographic departure.
- **A2 Mineral:** lighter sans hero, white surfaces, green action/selected-row
  rule/mark, neutral hypothesis status. Framed screens and smaller contextual
  heading make this the quietest lower-view hierarchy.
- **A3 Graphite:** achromatic dark surfaces, lighter sans hero, mono IDs,
  outlined action, neutral selected-row rule. Evidence remains visibly readable.

found. The literal finish prompts are only partially realized: A2's primary

exact adherence to every craft token. The selected prototype should resolve
