## Product boundary after release

v1.10.0 delivers repository-local root-cause intelligence over canonical
Markdown. It does not treat rank as proof, auto-confirm hypotheses, execute
fixes, create learnings from outcomes, require SQLite, aggregate repositories,
or add hosted state. Those boundaries remain governed by the central product
spec.
