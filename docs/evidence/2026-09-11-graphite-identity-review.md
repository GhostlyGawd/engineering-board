## Verdict

**Pass for presenting a bounded identity/type concept, with explicit design
limitations.** This is not production-asset approval, full prompt fidelity, or
evidence that the owner's concern about weak branding has been resolved.

The exact product name is readable as lowercase `engineering board` in the
stacked wordmark, horizontal specimen, small specimen, and application header.
The caption also spells `ENGINEERING BOARD` correctly. Brand lettering is the
strongest role; the display specimen, reading text, and mono metadata are
visually distinguishable. The bottom application strip remains subordinate to
the identity study.

The crop visibly preserves `Synthetic example`, `Read-only`, `Proposed
hypothesis`, and `No outcome recorded.` It does not portray the hypothesis as a
confirmed result. The source-record action is visible.

The image footer explicitly calls this an identity concept, not production
artwork. The README states that exact paths, font selection, licensing, metrics,
small-size legibility, themes, contrast, and interaction remain future checks.
Neither reviewed text establishes a production font or finished custom logo.

## Limitations and deviations

- The visible wordmark uses a single-storey `g`; it does not realize the prompt's
  requested distinctive double-storey `g`. Its letterforms still resemble a
  conventional heavy grotesk. Stronger scale and tighter setting alone do not
  establish an authored or distinctive identity.
- The secondary mark is three rounded horizontal blocks. It does not visibly
  implement the requested shared bridge across two columns, and remains close
  to the generic bars identified in the prompt as a weakness.
- Subtle texture and tonal variation remain in the raster, including the mark
  and application surfaces, despite the prompt's flat, texture-free direction.
- The `App header scale (24px)` caption is a specimen label, not verified font
  metrics. Inspection of this image does not establish legibility at actual
  application scale, responsive behavior, selectable text, or accessible
  contrast.
- The visual separation of roles is inspectable, but actual typeface identity,
  weight values, kerning, font licensing, vector quality, and reproducible
  implementation cannot be certified from this generated raster.

The owner can evaluate this specimen as a proposed direction. A claim that the
type is now sufficiently distinctive or premium requires their judgment;
production readiness requires actual font and vector assets plus implementation
verification.
