# Establish a small agent development team with independent evidence review

## Done when

- [ ] Roles and authority are linked from AGENTS.md and CONTRIBUTING.md.
- [ ] Assignment and review templates define scope, claim owner, verifier and evidence.
- [ ] Independent process review and D.1 v2 audit are recorded durably.
- [ ] Confirmed audit defects are canonical follow-ups; implementation and product outcomes remain distinct.

## Evidence

> Owner approved product owner, lead, builder and independent verifier roles, task-specific audit lenses, durable handoffs and evidence, and a first independent D.1 v2 audit before another live experiment. Complete when repository guidance defines decision rights, risk-based review, claim/worktree ownership, release gates and measures; reusable assignment/review templates exist; an independent reviewer assesses the process; and the first audit is recorded with canonical follow-up entries.

## Acceptance criteria

- Roles and decision authority are documented and linked from AGENTS.md and CONTRIBUTING.md.
- Assignments identify board owner, scope, expected behavior, reviewer and delivery evidence.
- Review lenses, escalation, durable evidence, and release verification have explicit handoffs.
- An independent process review and D.1 v2 audit are preserved; confirmed defects have canonical follow-up work.
- No product-effect claim or new evaluation baseline is inferred from process adoption.
