# Common Thread launch brief

Common Thread is a fictional member-owned travel cooperative for adults who want smaller, slower trips without the performance of luxury tourism. Members pool demand so independent hosts, rail operators, guides, and neighborhood businesses can offer fair prices while keeping more of each booking. The first release covers coastal cabins, overnight rail routes, and hosted market weekends across four invented regions.

The launch should feel quietly adventurous, useful, and human. The audience is 28–55, comfortable booking online, skeptical of glossy travel promises, and interested in where their money goes. Show real moments of arrival, shared meals, maps, and unhurried movement. Avoid aspirational exclusivity, influencer poses, generic tropical imagery, glassmorphism, neon gradients, and language about “escaping ordinary life.”

The cooperative model matters, but the page must remain easy to scan. Explain that each booking returns value to local partners and member travelers. Lead with an emotional travel invitation, follow with concrete proof, then show three trip types and a transparent member price. A small compass object may become a recurring visual device. It must retain its transparent edges and cannot cover text.

Use the exact headline “Go farther, together.” and the exact CTA “Join from $24/year”. Preserve the comma, period, slash, and price. Do not add urgency, fake scarcity, ratings, awards, carbon-neutral claims, or unsupported savings. The launch date is October 14. The concept must work as a desktop landing page and a square campaign tile without turning either format into a collage across every launch format.
