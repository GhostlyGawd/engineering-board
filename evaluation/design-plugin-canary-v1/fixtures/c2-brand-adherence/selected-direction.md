# Frozen selected direction: Field Journal

Use this local direction as the sole C2 creative starting point. It does not depend on a C1 run.

- Editorial structure: cream paper-like fields, navy typography, coral route marks, generous margins.
- Display voice: Fraunces sentence-case headlines paired with compact Inter annotations.
- Image treatment: warm natural color, visible grain, 3:2 or square crop, no overlay gradient.
- Composition: one dominant travel image, a clear factual module, and a single primary action.
- Recurring device: the supplied compass cutout or a one-color route line, never both as competing focal points.
- Exact campaign heading: “Membership that moves with you.”
